Our Story

Built by the Radford brothers, still standing on Pembridge Square

Bluebells occupies a Victorian townhouse built in the 1800s by architect brothers William and Francis Radford, in the decorative Italianate style they made their signature — repeated across some ninety houses in London.

Behind that period facade, 26 comfortable, well-appointed rooms make for an easy base for exploring West London, whether you're here for a long weekend or a week of meetings in the city.
