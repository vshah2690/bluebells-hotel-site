Frequently Asked Questions

We've got the answers. If you can't find what you need here, just get in touch.

What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in is from 3pm, and check-out is by 11am. Please leave your key at reception when you go.
Is breakfast included?
Yes — a continental breakfast is included with every room rate and served each morning downstairs.
Is there Wi-Fi at the hotel?
Complimentary wireless access is available throughout the hotel, on every floor.
Is there parking at the hotel?
We don't have on-site parking, but paid on-street parking is available right outside on Pembridge Square. Parkopedia is a good place to check nearby options in advance.
Can I bring my pet?
Well-behaved pets are welcome by prior arrangement — just let us know when you book so we can prepare a suitable room.
Is there luggage storage?
Yes, we're happy to hold your luggage before check-in or after check-out. Just ask at reception.
Can I smoke in my room?
No — all rooms at Bluebells are non-smoking. Guests found smoking in their room may be asked to vacate and charged a cleaning fee.
Do you provide baby cots?
We're happy to provide a travel cot in your room. Please contact us in advance to arrange one if it wasn't included with your booking.
Is the hotel accessible?
The building is an 18th-century townhouse without a lift, and there are a few steps at the entrance. None of our ground-floor rooms are specially adapted, so please contact us in advance if you have specific accessibility needs and we'll do what we can to help.
What's the nearest tube station?
Notting Hill Gate station is around four minutes' walk from the hotel, with the Central, District and Circle lines all stopping there.
What are the nearest airports, and how far are they?
Allow extra time during rush hour or bad weather. Roughly: London Heathrow (14 miles), London City (12.5 miles), London Luton (32 miles), London Stansted (47.5 miles), London Gatwick (54 miles).
Is there an ATM nearby?
There's a cash machine a short walk away on Notting Hill Gate — turn right out of the hotel and it's about two minutes on.
Is there a supermarket close by?
Yes, there are a couple of small supermarkets within a two to three minute walk along Notting Hill Gate and Pembridge Road.
Where's the nearest pharmacy?
There's a pharmacy on Pembridge Road, about a two minute walk from the hotel.
How often is my room serviced?
We'll service your room daily unless you'd prefer otherwise — just let reception know your preference. If you need extra toiletries, towels or tea and coffee in the meantime, just ask.
What toiletries are supplied?
We provide shampoo, shower gel and soap in every room. If you've forgotten anything else — a toothbrush, for instance — just ask at reception and we'll try to help.
I need to amend my reservation — what do I do?
If you booked directly with us, just get in touch and we'll help where we can. If you booked through a third-party site, you'll need to contact them directly, as any changes are subject to their terms.
What happens if I need a doctor?
If you need medical assistance during your stay, please let reception know and we can help you find the right support nearby.
Can you help with my bags?
Of course — just ask a member of staff and we'll be glad to help.
Is there a car rental nearby?
There's a car rental branch a short walk from the hotel on Uxbridge Street.

Still have a question? Get in touch and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
